# World 6: Decisions & Discipline (Planned)

**Big idea:** Good decisions are made before the outcome is known. Plan ahead, weigh risk with reward, know the limits of tools like stop orders, choose to wait when unsure, and learn honestly from losses.

## Learning objectives

| | Grades 3–5 | Grades 6–8 |
|---|---|---|
| Uncertainty | Say "it might" instead of "it will"; list more than one possible outcome. | Separate a good decision from a good outcome; describe ranges of outcomes. |
| Planning | Make a simple plan: what I'll do if the price goes up, and if it goes down. | Write a plan with a reason, a risk limit and what would change my mind. |
| Stop orders | A stop order is a plan for getting out, not a promise of the price. | A stop becomes a market order at the stop price; the fill can be much worse in a fast market (Investor.gov); stop-limit may not fill. |
| Patience | Waiting is a real choice. | Opportunity cost of acting vs. waiting; avoiding impulsive decisions. |
| Reflection | Say what I learned from a mistake. | Decision journal: prediction, reasoning, outcome, lesson. |

## Prerequisite knowledge

w2l1 (futures-risk), World 5 (gains/losses, margin).

## Vocabulary

uncertainty, risk, reward, stop order, plan, decision journal.

## Required visuals

Scenario tree (price up / flat / down); stop order "gap" diagram showing a fill below the stop price; decision-journal template (HTML).

## Practice question ideas

Good decision vs. good outcome sorting; "what's missing from this plan?"; stop-order mistake detective ("my stop guarantees I sell at $50"); reflection prompts in Thinking Lab and the Decision Journal.

## Assessment plan

Lesson checks; World 6 Test; Decision Journal entries reviewed by learner (not scored for profit); Thinking Lab rewards reasoning and risk awareness, never pretend profit.

## Accuracy notes

- Stop order: "The stop price is not the guaranteed execution price" (Investor.gov bulletin, verified); CFTC Glossary: a stop order "becomes a market order when a particular price level is reached" (verified).
- Risk = degree of uncertainty and/or potential financial loss (Investor.gov, verified).
- No leaderboards, earnings rankings or profit rewards in this world.
