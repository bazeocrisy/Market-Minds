# World 3: What People Trade (Planned)

**Big idea:** Futures exist for many kinds of things: farm products, metals, energy and stock indexes. A futures contract is different from owning a stock.

## Learning objectives

| | Grades 3–5 | Grades 6–8 |
|---|---|---|
| Commodities | Name farm, metal and energy things people trade; say who uses them (bakers, jewelers, drivers). | Define commodity; connect each commodity to producers and users who face price risk. |
| Stock indexes | An index is like a class average for a group of companies. | An index measures the performance of a basket of stocks (Investor.gov); index futures track an index, not one company. |
| Futures vs. stocks | A stock is a small piece of a company; a futures contract is an agreement about a future trade. | Ownership vs. agreement; standardized size; both can lose value; leverage in futures (preview of World 5). |
