# Asset Manifest (Art Requests)

**Status: NONE of the files listed below exist yet.** The app shows clearly named placeholders (the `image` visual kind in `js/ui/visuals.js`) until approved art is added. Do not substitute stock photos, AI images cut from mockups, or invented illustrations. All lesson text and buttons stay in real UI, never baked into pictures.

## Style for every illustration

- Polished, semi-realistic, warm and respectful; Modern Learning Academy palette (navy #102541, teal #168D86, gold #F0BF55, cream #F7F8F5) used as accents.
- Fictional, everyday Black children, families and professionals with varied skin tones, hair textures and styles (braids, locs, twists, afros, fades, head wraps), ages, body types and family structures (grandparent-led, single parent, aunt/uncle, two-parent). No stereotypes, no caricature.
- No text, prices or logos inside the art (prices are shown by the app). No real brands.
- Format: `.webp`, at least 1600 px wide for 16:9 and 1200 px wide for 4:3.
